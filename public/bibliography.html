References

Bibliography

All studies cited across the research and programme pages of this site, listed alphabetically by first author. Tags indicate which section or practice each reference supports.

Every claim on this site is tied to a specific study or established body of evidence. Where research findings are mixed or limited — particularly for speech and fatigue — this is stated explicitly on the relevant pages. References are formatted in APA 7th edition. DOI links are provided where available. Please verify currency before citing in clinical or academic work.

If you identify an error in a reference, please use the contact page to notify us. Accurate attribution matters.
