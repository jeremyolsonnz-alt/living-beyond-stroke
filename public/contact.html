About the site's author

Jeremy Olson holds a Master of Social Sciences (Psychology) from the University of Waikato, New Zealand, with graduate-level quality-of-life research conducted at Deakin University under Professor Robert Cummins. He also holds a Diploma of Solution-Oriented Hypnosis with Dr Robert McNeilly.

Living Beyond Stroke was developed from the same research and educational framework as Living Beyond SCI — a free resource for people with spinal cord injury. Jeremy does not have a stroke history; the programme is offered as an educator working in this field, not as someone with lived experience of the condition.

The programme is free and will remain free. There is no advertising, no affiliation, and no commercial interest in any of the products or approaches described.
