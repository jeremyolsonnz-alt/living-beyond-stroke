For Media

Jeremy Olson is a psychology researcher in Waihī, New Zealand, with an MSocSc from the University of Waikato. After losing his father to stroke and watching family members live with Parkinson's, he created Living Beyond: free guided imagery websites for people living with stroke, Parkinson's and spinal cord injury.
